HOW TO USE
The three CSV headers exactly match the contractor templates. All blank cells remain blank. Engineering columns are untouched. Sources name the PDF viewer page, printed revision, and chosen variant or assumption.
New rows are model/variant research records. Complete configured ordering codes are absent from the supplied documents; no orderable code was invented. Supply selections are provisional and explicitly marked assumed. New models are absent from the supplied worklist, but absence from the complete 329-part library cannot be checked because that library was not supplied.
All physical switch ports are inventoried under the user instruction to assume every port configured and available. One complete fiber-rich layout is chosen for each switch; mutually exclusive media options are not added together. Management ports are separate groups and are not ordinary forwarding capacity. SFP cages retain kind sfp_slot and blank media per template, even when assumed fitted.
Front management speeds on SEL-2731, SEL-2740S and SEL-2741 could not be specifically verified in supplied documents or the targeted manufacturer search. Their physical ports are included with speed blank and explicit source notes. These three groups require a confirmed speed before importer validation.
SEL-2488 and SEL-2730M-EXT now contain full device records as requested. SEL-2488 retains the supplied worked-example50 W planning allowance, with its assumed basis explicit. Its description corrects the earlier SFP wording: rear Ethernet interfaces are fixed RJ45 or LC options, not SFP cages.
Clock Ethernet and timing ports are documented in the SEL-2488 description/source notes. The contractor schema reserves device_ports.csv for switches and firewalls, so a clock is not entered as a switch.
Power values for selected dual-supply configurations may be conservative sums of published input burden ceilings. They are identified as computed allowances, not measured consumption; redundant sharing can make actual draw lower.
The full current library was not supplied. Values have page-level sources, but comparison and merged cross-field validation against that library remain outstanding. The generator/importer was not supplied, so its actual dry run was not executed.
